How these deals were checked

Every price on this page was read on Friday 27 November 2026 (15:35 WIB) from a deals source that names the store and the price — MacRumors, CNN Underscored, The Gadgeteer, Digital Citizen, DealNews, AppleInsider and others — or from the store's own product page. Each deal card names its source. Store links go to the store's own product page for that exact product wherever one was verified; the rest go to the store's search page for that product.

Each deal carries an honest verdict: NEW LOW means the sources report the lowest price ever for that product; STRONG DEAL means a genuine large cut; ROUTINE SALE means a real but modest cut. Prices move all day on Black Friday — check the price at the store before you buy.
